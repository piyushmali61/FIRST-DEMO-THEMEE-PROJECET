Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a Bonafide Certificate?

Submit a request through the Student Portal or visit Student Section Window No. 3 with your college ID card. Processing takes 24 working hours.

What is the minimum attendance requirement?

As per university and college autonomous norms, a minimum of 75% attendance is compulsory in lectures and practicals to be eligible for exams.

How do I renew books in the library?

Books can be renewed twice via the Library counter or the online Web-OPAC portal before the due date stamp.

Where can I pay semester and hostel fees?

Online payment can be completed via NetBanking/UPI in the Fees tab, or at the college Accounts Counter via DD/Card.
